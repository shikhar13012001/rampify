# Rampcut

## Register

product

## Users

Video creators and editors making short clips and repeatable client deliverables in a browser. They need precise speed changes, a readable preview, and reliable downloadable files.

## Product Purpose

Edit speed curves, arrange clips, add music and captions, and export locally. Footage stays on the user's device. The editor is the primary product surface; marketing supports that workflow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, practical. Preserve the established warm light identity confirmed by the user on 2026-10-20.

## Anti-references

Raw browser forms mixed with styled controls, decorative dashboard chrome, illegible low-contrast labels, and settings that compete with the video and curve editor.

## Design Principles

- Keep footage and curve editing at the center of the workspace.
- Make actions, selected states, and editable values immediately recognizable.
- Use the same control vocabulary throughout the editor.
- Explain local saving, relinking, export usage, and processing accurately.
- Keep dense controls usable on narrow screens and with a keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, persistent labels, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives. Existing CSS tokens and the current Clay reference describe the visual identity; the older dark-theme inventory in DESIGN.md is historical.
